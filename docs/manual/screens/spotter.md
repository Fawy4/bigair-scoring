# Spotter screen

The spotter's phone at /spot/‹event›: log every attempt — which rider, which trick, landed or CRASH — the moment it happens.

Last checked: 17 Oct 2026 · Product version 0.24.4

## What it is for {#sp-purpose}

What the spotter logs is what the judges score: each attempt appears on every judge's phone within about a second. The screen opens the running heat by itself and shows the next heat between heats.

Since 0.24.0 the spotter has **one view: the Rider sheet** (one numbered line per attempt; a line logs itself when you pick the trick or turn on Crash). There is no switch: a phone that chose the old Trick list before also opens on the Rider sheet, also after a reload. (The Trick list is still in the product's code, hidden, and can come back later; no setting or address on the live site opens it.)

## The Rider sheet {#sp-rider-sheet}

Like the judges' Rider sheet, but the spotter fills in the lines and **nothing needs a Log press**.

![The spotter's Rider sheet](../img/spotter-rider-sheet-390.png)
*spotter-rider-sheet-390.png — three attempts logged (the third a crash), line 4 being typed with its suggestion under the box.*

- **The rider cards** (Rider label with the colour word, name, nationality, “n / max” attempts) sit across the top. Tap a rider to show that rider's sheet. Assigned riders come first; **Other riders** shows the rest.
- **The lines.** One numbered line per attempt, empty until it is filled in: as many lines as the division's attempt limit (7 for Arrow). With **no limit** there are 10 lines from the start and an **Add attempt** button under the last line adds one more line each time it is pressed.
- **Each line** reads, from the left: the attempt number, one small column left of the trick box with two square boxes **L** and **R** over **Crash** (at least 32 px tall, the full width of the column; only one of L and R can be on, the chosen box is teal with a tick; tapping the other switches; the one that is on has a thick border and a tick), a wide **trick box** (type or speak the trick) with the microphone inside it while it is empty and a **×** that empties it in one tap once it has text, a **Crash** pill that is quiet at rest (a red outline, the icon and the word) and solid red with a tick and a ring when it is on, and its state in words: **Empty**, **Saving…**, **Logged ✓**. A line with a trick typed whose suggestions are open says **Tap a trick to log**.
- **Tidy names.** The first letter of a trick is a capital (the phone's keyboard starts with a capital too): “backroll” becomes “Backroll”. A one- or two-digit number at the start is a **count** written in front of the trick, in the vocabulary's own spelling and in the singular: “6 front rolls” becomes “6x Frontroll”, “2 backrolls” “2x Backroll”, “3x kiteloop” “3x Kiteloop”. A number of three digits (“720”) is not a count. A number that is part of a trick's own name is not a count either: “36 Kung-Fu HP” and “36 Kung-Fu 2 HP” are those tricks (0.24.4). If the rest matches no trick, the count still goes in front of the free text (“6x Zzzflip”). The same tidying happens in the Trick list's text box.
- **Emptying the box.** The **×** (or deleting the words) does nothing to an attempt that is already logged: it keeps its trick, and the box stays empty until you log a new trick. Use **Undo** (the first 10 seconds) or ask the head judge to remove an attempt.
- **Two ways to speak a trick.** (1) **The app's microphone**: it sits inside the trick box of a line that is empty (the **×** takes its place once there is text, so the box keeps its width). Tap it: **Listening…** shows at once; say the trick; the words go into the box as if you had typed them (the same tidy names, the same “6x Frontroll”, the same rule that the line logs itself). Tap it again while it listens to stop. It works only where the browser offers speech recognition, so the button is missing otherwise. (2) **The keyboard's own dictation key** (the microphone on the iPhone keyboard) in the trick box: it types the words for you and works wherever the keyboard does.
- **When the microphone does not answer** it never stays silent: one line says why, and under it, in small print, the browser's own word for the error (send a screenshot of it if you ask for help). **The microphone is blocked**: allow it on the iPhone (Settings → Safari → Microphone → Allow; for the home-screen icon also Settings → the app's name), then reload the page. **Voice is not available here**: this browser, or this phone's setting, has no speech recognition; type the trick or use the keyboard's dictation key. **Nothing heard**: say it again, closer to the phone. **No answer after 8 seconds**: the browser neither heard nor refused; the app stops it; tap the microphone again. The microphone also stops by itself when you change line, rider or view, and before a sound of the app plays.
- **No zoom when typing.** The text in every box is at least 16 px (a long trick name that is not being typed in is shown shrunk to fit, down to 12 px, over the box, which keeps its 16 px), so the iPhone no longer zooms in when you tap a trick box. Zooming itself is never blocked. There is also no Note button on this screen.
- **The rider cards stay at the top.** When you scroll down to the lower lines, the rider cards stay under the header, so you can switch rider from any line.
- **Only the first empty line takes typing.** The lines below wait (their boxes and buttons are greyed) until the line above is logged.
- **How a line logs itself.** As soon as Crash is on, or when the trick is entered. **A tapped suggestion only fills in the word you are writing and adds a space**: the keyboard stays open and the cursor stays at the end, so you keep writing (type “doub”, tap the suggestion, type “fro”, tap **Frontroll**, type “board”, tap **Board-off**). The line then logs itself after about **5 seconds** without typing, or as soon as you leave the box. A last word that is still unfinished shows **Tap a trick to log** while you write it. “board”, “board off” and “boardoff” all offer **Board-off**. Words that match nothing stay free text and are flagged for the head judge (the line says “Free text: the head judge will check it”). **L** and **R** can be set before or after; L or R alone is not an attempt. There is no Log button; the keyboard's Done closes the keyboard, which also logs what is in the box. The line then becomes that rider's next attempt and reaches the judges and the console exactly like any attempt.
- **Changing a line afterwards.** Every later change to a logged line (the trick, L / R, Crash) updates the **same** attempt within a second on the judges' phones and the console, never a second attempt. The judges' scores stay on the attempt, as when the head judge edits one. You can change the attempts **your own seat** logged until the heat is published; every change leaves a line in the audit log in words (“Spotter changed Red 3: Backroll Left → Frontroll Left”). Emptying a trick box does **not** delete the attempt: use **Undo** (the first 10 seconds) or ask the head judge to delete it in the console.
- **Crash.** Turning Crash on logs the line at once as a crash, as a crash always did: the judges' line greys and takes no score. The line shows **CRASH** in place of the trick, with the typed trick kept small under it (“typed: Frontroll”), so a rider's dispute can be checked later. A crash needs no trick. Turning Crash off makes it a normal attempt again.
- **Undo.** For the first 10 seconds after a line logs (Saving… or Logged ✓), a small **Undo** button appears under it, on the line that was just logged and only on an attempt **your own seat** logged. One tap takes the attempt back: the line becomes empty again and the judges' line for it goes (a score a judge had already typed on it goes back to waiting, as when the head judge deletes an attempt). It is the same rule as before (10 seconds, your own attempt) and the audit log says “attempt undone”. After 10 seconds: “Too late to undo: ask the head judge to delete it.”
- **Attempts logged by anyone** (a second spotter, the head judge) show in their lines, live and in order, labelled “Logged by another seat”; they cannot be changed from this phone, and the next empty line is always the next attempt.
- **The keyboard.** While the keyboard is open the whole screen is as tall as what the keyboard leaves free: the line being typed, with its L, R and Crash and its suggestions, moves up to sit just above the keyboard, so you see what you type.

### The New look {#sp-new-look}

Since 0.21.0 the spotter's screens wear the product's new look (see [The New look](judge.md#ju-new-look) on the judge page): rows with thin dividers, pill buttons, Rubik, Daylight by default. On an iPhone 14 in **Normal** the header, the rider cards and all **7 lines** fit without scrolling. The row of settings that used to sit at the bottom is now the one round **Settings** button at the end of the header (Daylight / Dark, Normal / Large, Sound). The trick box takes all the width that is left (at least 20 characters of a trick name show); the microphone shows while the box is empty and the × only while it is in use. Each line is one row: a small teal tick by its number says it is logged (the words **Empty**, **Saving…** and **Logged ✓** are kept for a screen reader and show only when something needs saying, such as a typed trick waiting for a tap); the typed trick is in Medium weight so it stays readable.

## Controls {#sp-controls}

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Header and **flag strip** | The clock line is the **flag strip** (flags on): the flag's colour, the state's words, the countdown and the heat name. The logger opens at **green** (Running), not at the yellow; at the last minute it stays open; at red (Finished) it closes as before. It covers none of the buttons ([Flags](flags.md)). |
| Rider cards | One Rider label per rider with attempts used (“5 / 7”). A rider who used every attempt turns grey: “Out of attempts · 7 / 7”, and the lines are off for them. Assigned riders first when the spotter is assigned. |
| **L** / **R** | Two small boxes on a line, over **Crash**; only one can be on; the chosen one is teal with a tick. |
| Trick box | Type or speak the trick (“left double backroll”): suggestions come from the division's Trick base; an unknown word is kept as free text for the head judge. The line logs itself. |
| **Crash** | Turning it on logs the line at once as a crash. |
| **Undo** | Under a line for 10 seconds after it logs: takes the attempt back. After that “Too late to undo: ask the head judge to delete it.” |
| **Feed** | What has been logged in this heat; “Possible duplicate” when two spotters logged the same rider within the duplicate window. |
| Connection badge | Synced / Pending / Offline / Failed — tap to retry, as on the judge screen. |

## What it depends on {#sp-depends}

The heat must be running (“The heat is not running, so nothing can be logged.”; paused: “Paused — logging is off until the head judge resumes.”). The rider must have attempts left (the division's cap; past the cap only the head judge adds one, with a reason). The trick names come from the division's Trick base. Judges can log too when the Event step allows it.
